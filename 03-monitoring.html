Pillar 03

Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

The feedback loop that keeps the COE's practices honest and current.

You're browsing as a Viewer (read-only). Log In in the top-right with your Microsoft account to upload documents, edit tags, or update story status.

Tracks industry trends, captures lessons learned, reviews test artifacts before they ship, standardizes monitoring practices and reporting, runs the continuous-improvement cycle, and analyzes defect trends across releases.

Deliverables (03.1 – 03.6)
3.1

Market Study & Trend Analysis

Owner: COE Member

Research testing trends and benchmarks in ERP QA, especially AI/ML, so practices stay aligned with industry standards.

Deliverable

Quarterly trend reports with recommendations to the COE.

Acceptance criteria

  • Quarterly trend reports published
  • Recommendations submitted to COE
3.2

Lessons Learnt

Owner: QA Lead

Capture and publish testing lessons learned from each release so future projects benefit from past experience.

Deliverable

Lessons-learned template and post-release retrospective log.

Acceptance criteria

  • Template created and circulated
  • Shared retrospective after each major release
3.3

Review Process

Owner: QA Lead

A review process for test cases and automation scripts so the COE maintains high-quality standards.

Deliverable

Review checklist and mandatory pre-UAT review gate.

Acceptance criteria

  • Review checklist in place
  • Mandatory review before UAT
3.4

Monitoring & Governance Practices

Owner: QA Manager

Guidelines, SOPs, templates and supporting documentation for regular monitoring practices such as dashboards, DSRs/WSRs and control charts.

Deliverable

Monitoring SOPs, DSR/WSR/dashboard templates, alerting configuration.

Acceptance criteria

  • Guidelines & SOPs defined on monitoring practices
  • Reports automated and scheduled
  • Templates defined for DSRs, WSRs, dashboards and other reporting needs
  • Alert mechanism configured and documented
3.5

Continuous Improvement

Owner: COE Lead

A continuous-improvement cycle based on feedback loops, retrospectives and metrics so QA maturity evolves over time.

Deliverable

Continuous improvement framework and tracked action log.

Acceptance criteria

  • CI log maintained and updated
  • Action items tracked to closure
3.6

Defect Trend Analysis

Owner: QA Lead

A standardized approach for capturing, analyzing and reporting defect trends across releases, with dashboards and RCA formats.

Deliverable

Defect trend methodology, dashboard templates, RCA format.

Acceptance criteria

  • Trend analysis methodology and documentation created
  • Dashboard templates for tracking defect types, severity and module-wise breakdown
  • RCA formats and guidelines published and adopted by project teams
