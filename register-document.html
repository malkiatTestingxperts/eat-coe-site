How this works

Fill in the details below, click Choose file to attach it from your computer, then hit Send — the email is sent immediately, straight from this page, as you (via your signed-in Microsoft account), with the file genuinely attached. No mail app opens, and there's nothing further to do — the COE team receives it and files it into the right SharePoint folder based on the pillar/labels you chose. (Files over 3MB can't be attached automatically — you'll be told if that happens, so you can share it separately.)
