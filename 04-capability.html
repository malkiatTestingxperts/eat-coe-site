Pillar 04

Capability Building & Enablement

Growing the people, knowledge and domain depth behind every engagement.

Builds the case-study library, the capability deck used to onboard stakeholders, skill matrices and training roadmaps, the central knowledge repository, business/domain knowledge assets, an SME network, mentoring programs and ERP-specific scenario libraries.

Deliverables (04.1 – 04.8)
4.1

Success & Failure Reports (Case Studies)

Owner: COE Member

Case studies of successful ERP testing engagements so the COE can showcase learnings and best practices.

Deliverable

Quarterly case study, reviewed and published internally.

Acceptance criteria

  • At least one case study per quarter
  • Reviewed and published internally
4.2

Capability Deck

Owner: QA Lead

A capability deck showcasing ERP QA strengths, services and assets so the COE can onboard stakeholders.

Deliverable

Capability deck, refreshed quarterly.

Acceptance criteria

  • PPT deck created and circulated
  • Updated quarterly
4.3

Skill Development

Owner: COE Member

Role-wise skill matrices and a training roadmap for ERP QA roles so teams are future-ready.

Deliverable

Skill matrix, learning paths, trainer roster, training calendar.

Acceptance criteria

  • Skill matrix validated and maintained
  • Learning path defined
  • List of trainers identified
  • Training sessions scheduled
4.4

Knowledge Management Framework

Owner: COE Lead

A centralized knowledge repository so team members can access and share best practices easily.

Deliverable

Central repository with indexing and folder structure — the foundation this website implements.

Acceptance criteria

  • Central repository enabled
  • Clear indexing and folder structure for easy traceability
4.5

Business & Domain Knowledge

Owner: COE Member

Business process flows and domain-specific ERP use cases so learners gain the knowledge to test real-world scenarios efficiently.

Deliverable

Module synopsis pages indexed with links to related documents, SME walkthroughs.

Acceptance criteria

  • Synopsis page for each module with index and links to related documents
  • SME walkthrough sessions
4.6

Business / Domain SMEs

Owner: QA Director

Identify and nominate ERP domain SMEs across modules so project teams get timely support and guidance.

Deliverable

SME directory by module with a monthly sync cadence.

Acceptance criteria

  • SME list created
  • Monthly sync meetings
4.7

Career Growth & Mentoring

Owner: COE Lead

A structured L&D program so testers can continuously upgrade their skills.

Deliverable

Training curriculum with notes/presentations, attendance and feedback tracking.

Acceptance criteria

  • Monthly training cadence
  • Training curriculum designed with notes and presentations
  • Feedback and attendance tracked
4.8

ERP-Specific Scenarios & Use Cases

Owner: COE Member

Document use cases specific to ERP so testers can jump-start with generalized scenarios and ensure comprehensive coverage.

Deliverable

Business/functionality-specific standard scenario library, reviewed quarterly.

Acceptance criteria

  • Business/functionality specific standard use cases / scenarios
  • Usage documented
  • Capture feedback and incorporate improvements
  • Quarterly review and update of the repository
